PHILOSOPHY

My work exists at the intersection of data, visualization, and human perception—a liminal space where information transforms into understanding, much like how sunlight becomes life through photosynthesis.

I approach code not as mere instruction but as a living ecosystem. Each function, each module relates to others in a web of interdependence, creating emergent complexities from simple foundational elements. This perspective guides both my visualization work and application development.

"In nature, nothing exists alone."
— Rachel Carson

The monochromatic palette of this site reflects my design philosophy: constraint breeds creativity. By limiting variables, we see more clearly the essential patterns and relationships that might otherwise remain hidden beneath layers of distraction.

When I visualize data, I'm not simply plotting points or drawing lines—I'm revealing relationships that already exist but remain unseen until given form. Like a fallen log in a forest that becomes nurse to new saplings, data visualization transforms the decay of raw numbers into the growth of new understanding.
